## Intended Users

- Applicants preparing for NCEPU (Beijing) School of Nuclear Science and Engineering re-examinations
- Students reviewing metallic materials, metallurgy, or fundamentals of materials science
- Learners who need a quick structure for metal structures, phase diagrams, heat treatment, defects, properties, and corrosion

## Topic Coverage

According to the syllabus, the review scope includes:

- Metal structures: atomic bonding, typical crystal structures, amorphous state, crystal directions/planes, alloy phase structures
- Solidification and microstructure: pure-metal solidification, alloy concepts, ingot microstructures
- Phase diagrams: isomorphous, peritectic, eutectic, eutectoid, Fe-C phase diagram, lever rule
- Plastic deformation and recrystallization
- Solid-state phase transformation and heat treatment
- Crystal defects: point defects, dislocations, interfaces
- Mechanical properties: hardness, strength, creep, fatigue, impact properties
- Corrosion properties: uniform corrosion, galvanic corrosion, erosion/corrosion, stress corrosion

## Suggested Workflow

1. Read the syllabus first to define the review boundary.
2. Organize concepts by question type: definitions, fill-in-the-blank, short answer, and analysis.
3. Use the recalled-question PDF for self-testing.
4. Return to textbooks and lecture notes for diagrams, derivations, and examples.
5. Prepare a final cheat sheet for frequent definitions, phase diagrams, and heat-treatment processes.
